# Research protocol and model cards — 18 November 2026

**Question:** At equal retrospective retry counts, can a cheap predicted-improvement score outperform first-pass foreground entropy for breast-lesion delineation? This extends [Amiri et al.'s two-stage segmentation study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32350786/); it does not claim to invent two-stage segmentation. The unit of a routing decision is an image; the unit of statistical inference is a patient.

## Cohorts and endpoints

Develop on BUS-BRA folds 1–3. Fold 4 is for the oracle go/no-go decision and calibrating the streaming threshold, never a held-out performance claim. Fold 5 is internal test. BrEaST is the primary external endpoint; BUS-UCLM is a separate external replication. Develop on BUS-BRA only. Freeze the cohort rules and archive hashes before opening any external predictions.

Include all eligible scans in the budget. On lesion-positive frames calculate binary Dice after mapping both masks to native coordinates. Mean over frames per patient first and then across patients with at least one positive frame. For normals report the fraction with any predicted foreground; if there are no normal frames this is undefined. A completely missed **positive image** has zero overlap with its annotated lesion union. This is not a per-lesion detection sensitivity study. Report helped/harmed proportions among retried positives.

Primary contrast: predicted-gain minus entropy Dice on BrEaST at floor(20% × eligible scans) retries. Use 2,000 paired bootstrap resamples of patient-level differences (seed 2411). Keep assignments fixed across draws: the interval is conditional on this cohort's ranking, not uncertainty from retraining or policy recalibration. Support superiority only if the lower bound is above zero. Report other cohorts and 0/5/10/40/100% budgets descriptively; do not pool cohorts or move the primary endpoint.

## Model cards

**Prespecified secondary comparison (18 November 2026, before the GPU pilot):** compare predicted recovery against predicted first-pass difficulty on BrEaST, with the same patient-macro Dice endpoint, 20% all-scan budget, tie rule and paired patient bootstrap as the primary comparison. Both regressors use exactly the same OOF rows, first-pass features, hyperparameters and seed; only the target changes: signed second-minus-first Dice for recovery, versus one minus first-pass Dice for difficulty. Larger scores receive priority in both arms. Record the common, gain-only and difficulty-only retry counts; identical retry sets imply identical masks and zero contrast. This secondary interval is descriptive, not an alternative superiority claim. Fold-4 eligibility, the primary gain-versus-entropy contrast, and the source-calibrated streaming policy are unchanged.

- **Segmenters:** separate four-downsampling-stage U-Nets, base width 32, GroupNorm, ReLU, no external pretraining. Full image letterboxed to 256; second pass at 384. Padding does not contribute to loss, entropy, or Dice. Models use native grayscale intensities scaled to [0,1]. AdamW, learning rate 0.001, effective batch 8, equal per-image BCE + soft Dice, 50 fixed epochs, terminal checkpoint, seed 2411. No test-time augmentation.
- **Refiner:** at training time, independently jitter padding on the four sides of the reference bounding box uniformly from 5% to 45%; use full-frame examples with 20% probability. At inference, take bounding box of all first-pass foreground (threshold 0.5), add 25% margin per side and clip to frame. Empty foreground triggers a full-frame fallback. Restore refined crop on zero background: no rule chooses between first and second based on the target. Cropping can worsen or lose a lesion.
- **Routing:** patient-disjoint OOF targets are signed changes in native-resolution Dice. Fit 60 squared-error boosting trees, depth 2, minimum leaf size 5, learning rate 0.05. Inputs: foreground fraction, 4-connected component count, largest-component fraction of full frame, crop fraction, mean foreground confidence (zero if empty), maximum probability, entropy, and empty-mask flag. The entropy baseline uses mean binary entropy on pixels with p ≥ 0.1 or maximum entropy when none qualify. No flipped forward pass or target-derived information as a routing input.
- **Controls:** never, always, deterministic seeded random ranking, entropy, predicted gain, and reference-aware oracle. Ties use stable image-ID hashes. The exact-budget oracle ranks signed image gains divided by that patient's number of positive frames, with normal frames assigned zero contribution to the primary endpoint. It may be forced to spend retries even when gains are negative. It is an upper bound, not deployable.
- **Pilot:** proceed only if oracle − entropy ≥ 0.01 absolute Dice and oracle > never retry on fold 4. For these two comparisons, differences within eight float64 ULPs at 1.0 (about 1.8e-15 Dice) are treated as equal: equality passes the headroom condition and fails the strict improvement condition. The unrounded observed differences are retained. Otherwise stop and release the headroom analysis. No post-hoc refiner change under the same protocol. Verify the pre-pilot source/manifest snapshot before reading fold-4 images; bind its SHA-256 and actual runtime versions to the gate result.
- **Streaming and timing:** calibrate a score threshold and tie cutoff on fold 4. Without examining an external batch the threshold may retry more or fewer than 20%. Report actual rates. Batch-one synchronized component times with cached paired outputs support cost replay. Separately execute the threshold policy end to end, including decoding and refinement only on selected frames. Distinguish measured streaming timings from replay estimates, which exclude ranking/IO. Record GPU sharing and do not extrapolate server timings to clinical hardware.

## Limitations and release

Annotation scope differs across sources; images with multiple lesions and absent masks need explicit handling. BUS-BRA is lesion-enriched. The external datasets do not establish clinical utility or deployment safety. BUS-UCLM is small at patient level; acquisition-flag exclusions reduce its population further. The source-calibrated router may fail out of domain. One random segmentation training seed limits inference about training variability. Synthetic demo outputs have no biomedical interpretation. Retain the frozen models privately and report a null result without retuning on external labels.
